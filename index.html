The problem

Professional networking
is broken.

Every platform rewards broadcasting over connecting. People post to be seen, not to say something true. Profiles become CVs. Connections become numbers. What starts as a network turns into an audience.

ROSTA is built the other way. Real people. Present tense. Warm introductions only. No performance required.
